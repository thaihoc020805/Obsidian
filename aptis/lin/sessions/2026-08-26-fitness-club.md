## Part 2 — running

> The last time I went for a run was on my Cat Ba trip last week. I woke up early at 5 a.m. and ran along the beach with my girlfriend. It was such a relaxing time.

Key learning:
- `go for a run`
- `on my trip to Cat Ba`
- `relaxing` describes the experience

## Part 3 — exercise routine

> I go to the gym three times a week with my PT, who teaches me how to exercise properly and gives me a detailed diet plan. I also do some activities such as swimming or playing badminton with my friends once a week for each activity.

Key learning:
- `three times a week`
- comma with non-defining `who`
- `such as + V-ing`

## Part 3 — morning running

Useful final pattern:

> To be honest, I usually run on a treadmill, but sometimes I go for a run in the morning. I don't do it very often because I can't wake up early, but I think running in the morning is much more refreshing and energizing than in the evening, especially after a tiring day at work.

Key learning:
- `treadmill`
- `not very often`, not `not much`, for frequency
- `refreshing` vs `refreshed`
- `energetic` vs `energizing`
- `much + comparative`

## Part 3 — technology and exercise

> I totally agree with this. I have had a lazy lifestyle and haven't exercised regularly for the past three years because I spend too much time on computers and phones. However, over the past three months, I have started focusing on my health by eating clean, going to the gym and playing sports.

Key learning:
- `for the past three years`
- `over the past three months`
- present perfect for a period continuing to now
