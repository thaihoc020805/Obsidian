# Aptis Writing Mistakes

Only recurring or high-value mistakes. Format: **wrong → right → why**.

## Frequency and time

- ❌ `three time a weeks` → ✅ `three times a week`
  - `three` requires plural `times`; the pattern is `a week`, not `a weeks`.

- ❌ `for the past three year` → ✅ `for the past three years`
  - plural after `three`.

- ❌ `I couldn't wake up early` when talking about a current habit → ✅ `I can't wake up early`
  - use present tense for a current/general problem.

## Word forms

- ❌ `more refresh` → ✅ `more refreshing` / `more refreshed`
  - `refresh` is a verb; use an adjective.

- ❌ `running is energetic` → ✅ `running is energizing` / `I feel energetic`
  - `energetic` usually describes a person; `energizing` describes something that gives energy.

## Verb patterns

- ❌ `such as go swimming or play badminton` → ✅ `such as swimming or playing badminton`
  - use nouns or V-ing after `such as`.

- ❌ `go for the gym` → ✅ `go to the gym`
  - fixed collocation.

## Agreement

- ❌ `my PT, who teach me` → ✅ `my PT, who teaches me`
  - `who` refers to singular `my PT`, so the verb takes `-s`.

- ❌ `who ... give me` → ✅ `who ... gives me`
  - same subject–verb agreement rule.

## Prepositions

- ❌ `in my Cat Ba trip` → ✅ `on my trip to Cat Ba`
  - use `on` with `trip` in this meaning.

## Clarity

- ❌ `It isn't much` when meaning “not frequently” → ✅ `I don't do it very often`
  - `much` is not a frequency adverb here.

- Avoid unclear elliptical comparisons such as `unlike in the evening` in Aptis.
  - Prefer: `unlike running in the evening` or `than in the evening`.
