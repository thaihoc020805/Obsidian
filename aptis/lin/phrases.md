# Aptis Writing Phrases

Reusable phrases and collocations from actual practice. Learn the **whole chunk**, not isolated words.

## Fitness / exercise

- **go for a run** — đi chạy bộ
- **go for a walk / take a walk** — đi dạo
- **go to the gym**
- **run on a treadmill**
- **exercise properly**
- **a detailed diet plan**
- **play badminton**
- **go swimming**
- **once a week / three times a week**
- **for each activity**

## Describing feelings

- **It was such a relaxing time.**
- **Running in the morning is refreshing.**
- **I feel refreshed and energetic.**
- **after a tiring day at work**

## Time and experience

- **last week**
- **on my trip to Cat Ba**
- **for the past three years**
- **over the past three months**

## Opinion / contrast

- **I totally agree with this.**
- **However, ...**
- **unlike running in the evening, ...**
- **I prefer ... because ...**

## Useful sentence patterns

- **The last time I went for a run was ...**
- **I don't do it very often because ...**
- **I have started focusing on my health by + V-ing ...**
- **My PT, who teaches me how to ..., also gives me ...**
- **I also do some activities such as + V-ing ...**

Keep this list small. Add only phrases that are reusable in future Aptis answers.
