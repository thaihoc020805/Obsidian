# Aptis Writing Progress

## Current focus

Practise **all Aptis Writing Parts 1–4**, one question at a time.

## Working style

- Stay on the current question until the user says **next**.
- Correct every English message first.
- Explain briefly **why** each correction is needed.
- Then answer the actual question or continue the practice.
- Prefer natural, reusable English over unnecessarily advanced wording.

## Current strengths

- Can generate relevant ideas quickly.
- Can write multi-sentence Part 2/3 answers with personal examples.
- Good instinct for asking about grammar patterns instead of memorizing corrections blindly.

## Current weaknesses to watch

- subject–verb agreement (`teach` → `teaches`, `give` → `gives`);
- singular/plural in frequency phrases (`three times a week`);
- verb forms after expressions like `such as`;
- adjective forms (`refreshing`, `refreshed`, `energetic`, `energizing`);
- prepositions/collocations (`on a trip`, `go to the gym`);
- tense choice when describing present habits vs past events;
- writing too much for the target word count.

## Targets by part

- **Part 1:** 1–5 words; simple and accurate.
- **Part 2:** practise around 30–45 words unless the current exercise requires otherwise.
- **Part 3:** practise around 30–60 words per response.
- **Part 4:** short informal email + longer formal email; focus on tone, structure, and reusable patterns.
