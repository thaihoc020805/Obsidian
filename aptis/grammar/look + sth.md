## 1. `look at` ⭐⭐⭐ (quan trọng nhất)

> **Nhìn vào**

- I'm looking **at** the mountains.
- Look **at** this photo.

👉 Khi bạn **hướng mắt vào một vật**, gần như luôn dùng **at**.

---

## 2. `look for`

> **Tìm kiếm**

- I'm looking **for** my phone.
- She is looking **for** a job.

Không phải "nhìn để tìm", mà là **tìm**.

---

## 3. `look after`

> **Chăm sóc**

- She looks **after** her grandmother.

---

## 4. `look into`

> **Điều tra / xem xét**

- The police are looking **into** the case.

---

## 5. `look through`

> **Lướt qua / xem qua**

- I looked **through** the report.

---

## 6. `look around / round`

> **Ngó xung quanh**

- We looked **around** the museum.
- I looked **around** and saw nobody.

---

## 7. `look over`

> **Kiểm tra nhanh**

- Could you look **over** my essay?

---

## 8. `look up`

Có hai nghĩa:

### Tra cứu

- I looked the word **up**.

### Khởi sắc

- Things are looking **up**.

---

## 9. `look down on`

> **Coi thường**

- Don't look **down on** other people.

---

## 10. `look up to`

> **Ngưỡng mộ**

- I look **up to** my father.
