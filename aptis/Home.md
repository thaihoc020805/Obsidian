# Aptis

Use this vault in **three layers**:

1. **Practice** — write answers, make mistakes, get feedback.
2. **Review** — only high-value things worth remembering.
3. **Reference** — detailed grammar notes when you need to look something up.

## Tutors

- [[lyn]] — general English tutor and review system.
- [[lin]] — Aptis Writing practice, corrections, patterns, and review.

## Review first

- [[grammar/quick-reference|Grammar quick reference]] — short rules that actually came up in practice.
- [[lin/mistakes|Writing mistakes]] — recurring/high-value mistakes from Aptis Writing.
- [[lin/phrases|Writing phrases]] — reusable chunks and collocations.
- [[lyn/phrases|Lyn phrases]] — phrases learned with Lyn.

## Reference

- [[grammar]] — detailed grammar index. Open only when you need a topic.
- [[writing]] — older Aptis Writing answers/examples; treat this as an archive, not something to memorize.

## Rule for adding notes

Do **not** save every typo or every interesting sentence.

Save something when at least one is true:
- the mistake repeats;
- the rule is easy to confuse;
- the phrase is reusable in Aptis;
- it improves clarity, tone, or naturalness;
- it should appear in a future review/quiz.

Aptis Writing: write first, then fix. Review the small lists, not the whole vault.
