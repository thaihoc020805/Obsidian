# Lin — Aptis Writing

This is Lin's workspace for **Aptis Writing Parts 1–4**.

## What goes here

- [[lin/mistakes|Mistakes]] — only repeated or high-value writing mistakes.
- [[lin/phrases|Phrases]] — reusable chunks, collocations, and sentence patterns.
- [[lin/progress|Progress]] — current strengths, weaknesses, and what to focus on next.
- [[lin/sessions|Sessions]] — compact session notes when a full practice set is worth saving.

## What does NOT go here

- every typo;
- every corrected sentence;
- every interesting word;
- long grammar explanations that already belong in [[grammar]].

## Workflow

1. You write freely.
2. Lin corrects the message first and explains why.
3. Lin answers the actual question.
4. Only useful/repeated items are saved here.
5. Grammar rules are also summarized in [[grammar/quick-reference|Grammar quick reference]].

Goal: a **small review system that stays useful**, not a dump of everything ever learned.
