Dear President/Manager,

  

My name is Duong, and I have been a dedicated member of the club for over two years. I am writing to share my thoughts and offer some suggestions regarding [A: vấn đề].

  

I was [B: cảm xúc] to hear that [C: nội dung đề]. In my opinion, the best option would be to [D: giải pháp chính].

  

Firstly, [E: lý do 1]. For example, [F: cách thực hiện/ví dụ]. This would [G: lợi ích 1]. Secondly, [H: lý do 2]. As a result, [I: lợi ích 2].

  

In addition, I suggest [J: giải pháp phụ]. By doing this, the club could [K: kết quả]. I believe this plan would be practical, effective and beneficial to our members.

  

I hope you will consider my suggestions.

Giải pháp

[động từ] + [đối tượng] + [chi tiết]

  

Ví dụ:

  

organise + free workshops + with the help of local experts

  

Lý do

it would + [lợi ích] + because + [nguyên nhân]

  

Ví dụ:

  

it would reach a wider audience because most young people use social media

  

Hai lợi ích

[lợi ích 1] + and + [lợi ích 2]

  

Ví dụ:

  

attract more people and improve the club’s reputation

  

Ví dụ cụ thể

[người/tổ chức] + could + [hành động]

  

Ví dụ:

  

local experts could provide practical advice

Giải pháp phụ

I suggest + V-ing + [đối tượng]

  

Ví dụ:

  

I suggest offering free entry to all participants.
