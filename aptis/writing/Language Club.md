Therefore, I am writing this email to share my thoughts and offer some suggestions regarding the upcoming event.

Regarding the theme, I suggest choosing "Effective ways to learn a foreign language". The speaker could discuss useful methods for enhancing speaking, reading, listening and writing skills. For example, members could learn how to practise speaking every day without getting bored, remember new vocabulary and use online resources effectively.

In addition, it would be a good idea to open a short answer-and-question session. By doing this, the club could encourage everyone to participate and learn from one another.
